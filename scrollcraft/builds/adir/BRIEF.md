# BRIEF: Adir Revah, personal site

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner answered the
interview with "use your judgment". Nothing below is a quotation from them.
Every line is an authored decision, and every fact about the person is limited
to what the repository shows: the site is called AdirSite and belongs to Adir
Revah. No job title, employer, client, project or number has been invented.
Copy that speaks for Adir (the five verbs and their glosses) is a draft in
their voice for them to keep, edit or replace.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Direct, warm, typographic, a little playful. References: a
   letterpress type specimen sheet, the title cards in a Wes Anderson film,
   Yves Klein's blue monochromes.
2. **Journey.** The name, very large. A short hello. Five words, one at a time,
   each with a one-line definition. A note of the words the visitor kept. A
   quiet sentence and a way to write to Adir.
3. **Energy.** Loud open (one word at viewport scale), then very quiet (small
   type, lots of air), then the loudest run on the page (the five words), then
   a medium echo, then the quietest screen on the site.
4. **Feeling and the moment.** Curiosity, then ease, then attention, then
   surprise, then warmth. The moment: the last line of the page is made of the
   words you stopped on.
5. **What no other site does.** It writes its last sentence from what you
   paused to read.
6. **Range.** Typographic poster, playful edge. Not premium-minimal: a light
   page that cuts to saturated cobalt and back.
7. **World.** Distinct scenes with hard cuts, not one continuous world.
8. **Assets.** None. No photos, no footage, no generation key. Type is the
   imagery. That is a reason for the grammar, not a compromise.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Curiosity   one word at 40vw on bone paper; the scroll pushes into the bowl of the "d" and blue floods out of it
2  Ease        a hello at reading size, a lot of blue space around it, one instruction in second person
3  Attention   five words at full viewport scale, the blue deepening under each; stop and the word is kept
4  Surprise    the page lists back exactly the words you stopped on, wiped in across the letterforms
5  Warmth      back on paper, the smallest type on the site: one sentence built from those words, and a link
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling.

## The peak

> I stopped on a word and the site kept it, and at the end it wrote me a sentence out of the ones I kept.

Lives in act 3 (collection) and pays off in acts 4 and 5. Act 3 has the largest
span on the page (5.2vh against 2.2 for the next largest).

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **the last line was written out of the words I paused on.**

## Authored silence

Act 2 (the hello) is the silence before the peak: small type on a lot of
bare blue. The first word of act 3 is already drawn as its stage slides in, so
the empty blue is never longer than the hello itself.

## Grammar and structure

- **Grammar:** Typographic poster (uniqueness.md 2.5).
- **Nav:** none. The only persistent chrome is a small "Kept" ledger at the
  lower lead corner that appears after the first word is kept.
- **Hero:** pinned scale-through. A ghost outline plane, the solid name and a
  cobalt iris plane, each scaling at a different rate around the bowl of the
  "d". Depth from scale and occlusion, no photography.
- **Close:** inverted to the quietest screen. One sentence, the name, the CTA as
  an underlined text link.
- **CTA label:** "Write to me". Used once, in one place.
- **Signature move:** dwell-to-keep. Holding still on a word for ~0.7s (or
  clicking it) keeps it. The ledger, act 4 and the closing sentence all read
  from the kept set.
